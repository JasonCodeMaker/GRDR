Multi-View Training Pipeline Fix — NLT=4 should beat NLT=1

models/grdr.py:204-214 reduces [B, N, D] to [B, D] via select(token_idx), leaving N-1 slots un-supervised by CE. Combined with multi-caption-per-video (pseudo on Panda; native on MSR-VTT), this produces per-row varying CE targets per video and the codebook collapse documented in package 2026-05-16-panda-pseudo-queries-multiview §2.5 (CanHit@100 = 44.59 vs anchor 95.57).

Identity

Problem
models/grdr.py:204-214 reduces [B, N, D] to [B, D] via select(token_idx), leaving N-1 slots un-supervised by CE. Combined with multi-caption-per-video (pseudo on Panda; native on MSR-VTT), this produces per-row varying CE targets per video and the codebook collapse documented in package 2026-05-16-panda-pseudo-queries-multiview §2.5 (CanHit@100 = 44.59 vs anchor 95.57).
Objective
On Panda 500K subset, train NLT=4 + Multi-View Tokenizer that beats NLT=1 baseline on CanHit@100 (single-seed, full 3-loop chain). Stretch goal: project winning recipe to 2.15M to beat production anchor (CanHit@100 = 95.57).
Motivation
Diagnostic package 2026-05-16-panda-pseudo-queries-multiview proved foundation cause is select-one-slot CE × per-row token_idx variance under multi-caption-per-video. The multi-view encoder structurally exists but is never exercised under retrieval-aware supervision — paper contribution #1 (multi-view tokenizer) is currently undefended. Fixing the training pipeline is the only path to recover the contribution.
